## 📐 Engineering Principles

- **Data Contracts First:** Define explicit schemas and validation rules before ingesting data.
- **Medallion & Modular Design:** Keep raw data untouched (Bronze), clean/enrich systematically (Silver), and aggregate for analytics (Gold).
- **Idempotency & Reproducibility:** Ensure all pipeline tasks are safe to re-run and backfill without duplicating or corrupting state.
- **Testing & Observability:** Automate unit tests, schema assertions, and pipeline health alerts.
